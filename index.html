🗾 Where Is Japan Located in Asia?

Japan is a country in East Asia with a total area of about 379,954 km². It is close to China, South Korea, Russia, and Taiwan. Japan is also part of the Pacific Ring of Fire and has five main islands: Hokkaido, Honshu, Shikoku, Kyushu, and Okinawa. These islands attract many tourists every year.

One thing that makes visitors want to come back to Japan is the local people. Japanese people are known for being friendly, helpful, and polite.

🌸 What Is the Best Time to Visit Japan?

Japan has four clear seasons because it is in a temperate climate zone. However, the weather can be very different from one area to another. For example, winters in northern Hokkaido can be very cold, while the islands in southern Okinawa are warm and sunny.

In general, March to May and September to November are considered good times to visit Japan.

If you want to see beautiful cherry blossoms and enjoy the cool weather after winter, visit Japan from late March to early April.

If you want to enjoy cool autumn weather, clear blue skies, soft sunlight, and colorful autumn leaves, late October to early November is a good time to visit.

🛂 Do You Need a Visa to Visit Japan?

Yes. Vietnamese citizens need a visa to visit Japan because Japan does not have a visa-free policy for Vietnamese citizens.

You need to apply for a tourist visa before your trip. According to the information in this guide, you only need to pay the visa fee after your visa application is successful.

✈️️ Tips for Booking a Flight to Japan

Plan your travel dates early and book your flight about one to two months before your trip. Choose a direct flight to save travel time.

  • Check special offers from different airlines.
  • Look for discounts and good deals.

Round-trip flights from Hanoi/HCMC to Tokyo range from 9,000,000 to 14,000,000 VND.

🚆 Transportation in Japan

1. Subway

The subway is affordable and on time. Be mindful of the morning and evening rush hour in Tokyo.

2. Bus & Passes

Use IC cards (Suica, Pasmo) or a One-Day Pass for convenience. A JR Pass is ideal for cross-city travel.

⛩️ Famous Places to Visit

  • Shibuya Crossing: View the busy scramble from the observatory deck at Shibuya Sky.
  • Mount Fuji: 3,776m tall, best viewed from Lake Kawaguchi.
  • Blue Cave, Okinawa: Famous for coral reefs and snorkeling.
  • Fushimi Inari Taisha: Walk through over 10,000 red torii gates.

🍣 Famous Japanese Food

  • Sushi: Prepared with seasoned vinegar rice and fresh seafood.
  • Sashimi: Fresh raw seafood served with wasabi.
  • Natto: Nutritious fermented soybeans with a unique aroma.

📅 Suggested 7-Day Itinerary

Day 1: Tokyo arrival ➔ Shinjuku & dinner.
Day 2: Asakusa, Tokyo Skytree, Harajuku & Shibuya.
Day 3: Mount Fuji (Kawaguchiko) & traditional ryokan.
Day 4: Shinkansen to Kyoto ➔ Fushimi Inari & Arashiyama.
Day 5: Nara Park deer ➔ Osaka.
Day 6: Universal Studios Japan or Dotonbori.
Day 7: Osaka shopping ➔ Kansai Airport departure.
