      <h2>🗾 Where Is Japan Located in Asia?</h2>

      <p>

        Japan is a country in <strong>East Asia</strong> with a <span class="vocab" data-word="total area">total area</span> of about 379,954 km². It is close to China, South Korea, Russia, and Taiwan. Japan is also part of the Pacific <span class="vocab" data-word="Ring of Fire">Ring of Fire</span> and has five main islands: <strong>Hokkaido, Honshu, Shikoku, Kyushu, and Okinawa</strong>. These islands <span class="vocab" data-word="attract">attract</span> many tourists every year.

      </p>

      <p>

        One thing that makes visitors want to come back to Japan is the <span class="vocab" data-word="local people">local people</span>. Japanese people are known for being <span class="vocab" data-word="friendly">friendly</span>, <span class="vocab" data-word="helpful">helpful</span>, and <span class="vocab" data-word="polite">polite</span>.

      </p>

    </section>


    <!-- Section 2 -->

    <section class="card">

      <h2>🌸 What Is the Best Time to Visit Japan?</h2>

      <p>

        Japan has four clear seasons because it is in a <span class="vocab" data-word="temperate climate">temperate climate</span> zone. However, the weather can be very different from one area to another. For example, winters in northern Hokkaido can be very cold, while the islands in southern Okinawa are warm and sunny.

      </p>

      <p>

        In general, <strong>March to May</strong> and <strong>September to November</strong> are considered good times to visit Japan.

      </p>

      <p>

        If you want to see beautiful <span class="vocab" data-word="cherry blossoms">cherry blossoms</span> and enjoy the cool weather after winter, visit Japan from <strong>late March to early April</strong>.

      </p>

      <p>

        If you want to enjoy cool autumn weather, clear blue skies, soft sunlight, and colorful <span class="vocab" data-word="autumn leaves">autumn leaves</span>, <strong>late October to early November</strong> is a good time to visit.

      </p>

    </section>


    <!-- Section 3 -->

    <section class="card">

      <h2>🛂 Do You Need a Visa to Visit Japan?</h2>

      <p>

        Yes. Vietnamese citizens need a visa to visit Japan because Japan does not have a <span class="vocab" data-word="visa-free policy">visa-free policy</span> for Vietnamese citizens.

      </p>

      <p>

        You need to <span class="vocab" data-word="apply for">apply for</span> a tourist visa before your trip. According to the information in this guide, you only need to pay the visa fee after your visa application is successful.

      </p>

    </section>

      <h2>✈️ Tips for Booking a Flight to Japan</h2>

      <p>

        There are many ways to find cheap flights to Japan. First, you should plan your travel dates early and book your flight about <strong>one to two months before your trip</strong>.

      </p>

      <p>

        You can also choose a <span class="vocab" data-word="direct flight">direct flight</span>. Direct flights may be more expensive, but they can save you time compared with flights that stop in another city.

      </p>

      <p>To find a good price, remember to:</p>

      <ul>

        <li>Check <span class="vocab" data-word="special offers">special offers</span> from different airlines.</li>

        <li>Look for <span class="vocab" data-word="discounts">discounts</span> and good deals.</li>

        <li>Compare prices before buying a ticket.</li>

        <li>Book your flight early.</li>

      </ul>

      <p>

        Popular airlines include <em>Vietnam Airlines, Vietjet Air, Japan Airlines, and ANA</em>. A round-trip flight from Hanoi to Tokyo may cost around 12,000,000 to 13,000,000 VND, while a round-trip from Ho Chi Minh City costs around 9,000,000 to 14,000,000 VND.

      </p>

      <h2>🚆 Transportation in Japan</h2>

      <h3>1. Subway</h3>

      <p>

        The subway is one of the most popular forms of <span class="vocab" data-word="public transportation">public transportation</span> in Japan because it is <span class="vocab" data-word="affordable">affordable</span>, clear, and usually <span class="vocab" data-word="on time">on time</span>. Pay attention to <span class="vocab" data-word="rush hour">rush hour</span> in Tokyo (7:30 - 9:00 a.m. & 5:00 - 7:00 p.m.).

      </p>


      <h3>2. Bus</h3>

      <p>

        There are two main types: <strong>local buses and long-distance buses</strong>. Most tourists use <span class="vocab" data-word="IC cards">IC cards</span> (such as Suica, PASMO, or ICOCA) because they make paying faster and easier. You can also purchase a <span class="vocab" data-word="One-Day Pass">One-Day Pass</span> for unlimited travel.

      </p>


      <h3>3. Taxi & Renting a Car</h3>

      <p>

        Compared with buses, taxis are quite expensive. <span class="vocab" data-word="Renting a car">Renting a car</span> gives you more freedom when traveling with close friends or family at your own pace.

      </p>


      <h3>JR Pass</h3>

      <p>

        With a <span class="vocab" data-word="JR Pass">JR Pass</span>, you can travel easily around Japan on JR train networks across multiple regions.

      </p>

      <h2>⛩️ Famous Places to Visit</h2>

      <ul>

        <li><strong>Shibuya Crossing, Tokyo:</strong> Famous scramble crossing with a lively view from <span class="vocab" data-word="observatory">Shibuya Sky</span>.</li>

        <li><strong>Mount Fuji:</strong> Japan's highest mountain (3,776m). Popular route: Yoshida Trail or view from Lake Kawaguchi.</li>

        <li><strong>Onna Village Blue Cave, Okinawa:</strong> A natural sea cave famous for <span class="vocab" data-word="snorkeling">diving and snorkeling</span>.</li>

        <li><strong>Taketomi Island, Okinawa:</strong> White sandy roads, red-roof houses, and water buffalo carts.</li>

        <li><strong>Shirakawa Village:</strong> Traditional houses with steep thatched roofs and historic architecture.</li>

        <li><strong>Tokyo Skytree:</strong> Impressive 634-meter tall landmark with panoramic city views.</li>

        <li><strong>Tokyo Imperial Palace:</strong> The serene residence of the Japanese Imperial Family.</li>

        <li><strong>Sensoji Temple:</strong> Tokyo's oldest temple with a vibrant 200m shopping street.</li>

        <li><strong>Osaka Castle:</strong> Famous green roof, white walls, and over 600 cherry trees.</li>

        <li><strong>Arashiyama Bamboo Forest, Kyoto:</strong> A peaceful atmosphere with towering green bamboo.</li>

        <li><strong>Todai-ji Temple, Nara:</strong> Giant bronze Buddha statue located in Nara Park among wild deer.</li>

        <li><strong>Himeji Castle:</strong> Nicknamed “White Heron Castle”, a UNESCO World Heritage Site.</li>

        <li><strong>Kobe Harborland:</strong> Scenic waterfront, Port Wheel, and romantic evening sea breezes.</li>

        <li><strong>Fushimi Inari Taisha:</strong> Famous shrine with over 10,000 vibrant red <span class="vocab" data-word="torii gates">torii gates</span>.</li>

      </ul>

    </section>

      <h2>🍣 Famous Japanese Food</h2>

      <p>

        Japanese food is known for fresh ingredients, careful presentation, and delicate flavors:

      </p>

      <ul>

        <li><strong>Sushi:</strong> Prepared with seasoned <span class="vocab" data-word="vinegar rice">vinegar rice</span> and fresh seafood.</li>

        <li><strong>Sashimi:</strong> Fresh <span class="vocab" data-word="raw seafood">raw seafood</span> served with wasabi and soy sauce.</li>

        <li><strong>Sake:</strong> Traditional brewed rice wine, served warm in winter and chilled in summer.</li>

        <li><strong>Ramen:</strong> Rich broth noodles with pork, eggs, and regional toppings.</li>

        <li><strong>Tempura:</strong> Seafood and vegetables coated in light batter and fried crispy.</li>

        <li><strong>Takoyaki:</strong> Crispy exterior, tender octopus balls from Osaka.</li>

        <li><strong>Natto:</strong> <span class="vocab" data-word="fermented soybeans">Fermented soybeans</span> with a distinct aroma and rich nutrition.</li>

        <li><strong>Miso Soup:</strong> Essential soup made with miso paste and dashi stock.</li>

      </ul>
